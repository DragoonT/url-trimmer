Trim Playlist Account Connector · Example

Connect your platform account

Use your browser session with the local Trim Playlist app.

1
Choose a platform

Start the connection from Trim URL.

2
Sign in in your browser

Enter your password on the platform’s own page.

3
Save the session locally

Return to Trim Playlist and use the saved session.

Supported platforms

YouTube · Facebook · Instagram · X / Twitter · TikTok · Twitch

No password is sent to Trim Playlist. Only the selected platform’s cookies are saved to your local app.
